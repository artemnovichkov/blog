# Support

Found a bug, have an idea or a question? Write to [apps@artemnovichkov.com](mailto:apps@artemnovichkov.com?subject=Accorduon%20Support). Your iPhone model and iOS version help a lot.

## FAQ

### I press a key, but there's no sound

Like on a real accordion, keys only sound while air flows. On iPhone Duo, fold and unfold the phone. On any iPhone, rock it like a steering wheel, drag the bellows in the middle with a finger, or turn on **Auto Air** to keep them full.

### How do I hold it?

Turn your iPhone sideways, or open iPhone Duo like a book. Your left thumb plays the keys, with low notes at the top, the way you see a real accordion from the front. Your right thumb plays the bass buttons: dark ones play a bass note, light ones play a chord. Prefer it the other way round? Swap sides in Settings.

### I'd rather not fold my phone that much

Rock the phone, drag the bellows with a finger, or turn on **Auto Air**. You get the same sound without moving the hinge.

### What are registers?

Register switches choose which reeds speak, like on a real accordion. On iPhone Duo, tap the switches above the keys; on other iPhones, the round symbol on the bellows. Master plays all reeds, Clarinet a single clean one, Musette three reeds beating together for that Parisian shimmer.

### What does Accorduon Pro include?

One purchase, no subscription: Bassoon, Bandoneon, Violin and Musette registers, the whole song library, and future Pro features at no extra cost. There are no ads, with or without Pro.

### I bought Pro, but it's locked on a new iPhone

Open the Pro screen by tapping a locked song or register, then tap **Restore Purchases**, signed in with the same Apple Account.
