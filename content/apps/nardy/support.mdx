# Support

Found a bug, have an idea or a question? Write to [apps@artemnovichkov.com](mailto:apps@artemnovichkov.com?subject=Nardy%20Support). Your iPhone model and iOS version help a lot.

## FAQ

### Which games can I play?

Long Narde, with no hitting, one checker holding a point and one checker off the head per turn, and Short Narde, classic backgammon with hitting, the bar, gammons and backgammons. The board only allows legal moves.

### How do I throw the dice?

Tap Throw or shake your iPhone. On iPhone Duo, hold it half open and snap the hinge. You can also throw from the dice widget on your Home Screen.

### Why doesn't my opponent say anything?

The computer's remarks need Apple Intelligence. Check that your iPhone supports it and it's turned on in Settings → Apple Intelligence & Siri. Without it the opponent plays just as well, quietly.

### What does Nardy Pro include?

One purchase, no subscription: the Hard opponent, unlimited hints (3 per game without Pro), Pro table themes and app icons. There are no ads, with or without Pro.

### I bought Pro, but it's locked on a new iPhone

Open the Pro screen from the menu and tap **Restore Purchases**, signed in with the same Apple Account.

### Do my stats move to a new iPhone?

Yes, if you're signed in to iCloud with the same Apple Account: stats, levels, streaks and achievements sync through iCloud and come back on a new iPhone or after a reinstall. Achievements are also kept in Game Center. If you play on two iPhones at the same time, the profile with more games wins.
